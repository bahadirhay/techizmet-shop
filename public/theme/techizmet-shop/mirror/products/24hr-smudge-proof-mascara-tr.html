Description

Experience long-lasting, flawless lashes with the 24HR Smudge-Proof Mascara. Designed for all-day wear, this mascara resists smudging and fading, ensuring your eyes stay defined from morning to night. Its expertly formulated brush delivers precise application, separating and volumizing each lash without clumping. Ideal for those seeking reliable, professional-quality results that maintain a fresh look throughout the day.

Key Features

1. 24-Hour Long Wear
Enjoy all-day, budge-free lashes! This mascara is formulated to stay intact for up to 24 hours — no fading, flaking, or touch-ups needed.

2. Smudge-Proof Technology
Sweat, humidity, and oily lids are no match. The advanced waterproof and smudge-resistant formula ensures clean, defined lashes that don’t smear.

3. Volumizing & Lengthening
Dual-action formula instantly lifts and separates lashes, giving you full-bodied volume and dramatic length from root to tip.

4. Precision Brush
Equipped with a specially designed brush that grabs every lash—even the tiny ones—for an even, clump-free application.

5. Lightweight, Flexible Hold
Lashes stay curled and lifted without feeling stiff or heavy. The formula allows your lashes to move naturally while maintaining definition.

6. Water-Resistant
Perfect for long days, workouts, or emotional moments. Resists water and tears while remaining easy to remove with an oil-based remover.

How to Use

1. Start with clean, dry lashes.
Make sure there is no oil or residue on your lashes for the best application.

2.Wiggle the wand at the base of your lashes.
Hold the brush horizontally and gently move it back and forth at the lash roots—this creates volume.

3. Sweep upward through the lashes.
Pull the brush up and out to coat lashes from base to tip. Apply in smooth strokes.

4. Layer as desired.
For a bolder look, apply 2–3 coats. Wait a few seconds between layers to avoid clumping.

5. Lower lashes (optional):
Use the tip of the wand or hold the brush vertically to gently apply to lower lashes.

6. To remove:
Use a bi-phase (oil-based) makeup remover or cleansing balm. Gently press and wipe—no rubbing needed.
